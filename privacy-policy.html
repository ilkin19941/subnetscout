SubNet Scout — Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

SubNet Scout is a free, open-source network scanning tool. This page explains what data the app does and does not collect.

What SubNet Scout does NOT do

  • It does not collect, store, or transmit your personal information.
  • It does not send your scan results (IP addresses, hostnames, device names found on your network) anywhere. All scan results stay on your device.
  • It does not require an account, sign-in, or any personal identifiers to function.

What SubNet Scout does

  • Update check: on startup, the app makes a network request to check whether a newer version is available. This request does not include any personal or scan data — only a basic version check.
  • Network scanning: the app's core function is to scan IP ranges you specify on your own local network, to discover devices, hostnames, and open ports. This data is processed locally and displayed only to you.

Permissions

SubNet Scout requests network access (internet and local network) solely to perform its stated function — scanning networks and checking for updates. No permission is used for any other purpose.

Third parties

SubNet Scout does not share any data with third parties, as it does not collect any data to begin with.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact us via the support link on the app's Microsoft Store listing page.
